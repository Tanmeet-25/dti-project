Honest Reporting & Model Generalization Context

Deployed Architecture: This client-side page runs the PyTorch Deep MLP (2,468 → 512 → 128 → 1, ReLU, dropout 0.3) exported to ONNX. It quotes exclusively the neural network’s measured ROC-AUC across our benchmark splits, never the Random Forest’s:

Regime Split Description Neural Net ROC-AUC PR-AUC Generalization Implication
1. Random Pairs Both drug and target observed during training 0.964 0.985 Optimistic warm-start ceiling (memorisation dominant)
2. Unseen Drugs Trained target, structurally novel compound 0.899 0.955 Virtual screening setting
3. Unseen Proteins Trained compound, novel target protein 0.851 0.924 Target profiling setting
4. Cold-Start Neither drug nor protein present in training 0.751 0.875 True de novo interaction generalisation

Target Presence: The chosen target protein appears in the Split 1 training set.

Chemical Similarity Context: Max Tanimoto similarity against the 30,000-drug training reference sample is displayed as structural context. However, we do not claim a calibrated per-band accuracy from it. On Split 2, the neural network’s measured ROC-AUC across similarity bins is non-monotonic (<0.4: 0.804, 0.4–0.6: 0.823, 0.6–0.8: 0.937, >0.8: 0.684). This anomaly remains unexplained, so similarity must be regarded as chemical distance, not as a guarantee of model certainty.

Operational Scope: The model was trained strictly to distinguish strong binders (≤ 100 nM) from confirmed weak binders (≥ 10 μM). Output probabilities serve as ranking hypotheses to prioritize candidates for experimental assay testing, not as physical proof of binding.
